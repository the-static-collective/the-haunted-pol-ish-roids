# The Haunted Pol-ish-roids

A living camera organism for the Static Collective.

It can **shoot** a photograph or **eat** an existing one, preserve the canonical source witness, and develop a six-up family of haunted descendants. Human choices shape fast residue, responsive mood, and slow camera identity over time.

The camera's personality should be felt rather than exposed as a stat sheet. Its compositional layer may become strange, fallible, secretive, or theatrical; its provenance layer may not lie.

Planned lineage mechanics include irreversible **Burn the Camera** succession through a single-use **Phoenix Egg** and reciprocal, asymmetric **Quantum Chrysalis** encounters between living cameras.

Status: **design/specification phase**. See issue #1 for the approved organism and design gate.
